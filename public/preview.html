Valrun · Flagship personal project

Is this work actually worth doing?

Valrun evaluates the economics of AI-agent work before execution. It maps the capabilities a task needs and checks observed service supply and route evidence. Known costs, explicit assumptions and unknowns stay separate. The result is a deterministic economic decision with source provenance, freshness and an auditable receipt.

REST, OpenAPI and read-only MCP expose the evidence. AI synthesis does not override the economics. Valrun does not autonomously claim jobs, bid, pay, use wallets or execute discovered services.

I originated Valrun and continue to direct it, defining its product thesis, use cases, economic rules and safety boundaries. I shape architecture and integrations, review behavior, and iterate with AI development agents.
